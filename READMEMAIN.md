# Earth & Hub

Earth & Hub is a digital platform designed to bridge the information gap in rural communities by connecting people with reliable information, resources, opportunities, government schemes and local services.

## Problem

Rural communities often struggle to access reliable information about education, healthcare, government schemes, scholarships, jobs and local resources. Community issues and development needs are also not systematically recorded or connected with relevant support.

## Solution

Earth & Hub provides a centralized platform where rural communities can access information, discover opportunities and resources, submit and track local issues, and provide feedback.

## Key Features

- Rural information and resources
- Government schemes and scholarships
- Education and employment opportunities
- Community issue reporting
- Issue tracking
- Tamil and English support
- Community feedback
- Local resource information

## Technologies Used

- Python
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
